# Fortress availability burn rate (6x)

- **Impact:** Sustained errors threaten the 28-day availability SLO.
- **Diagnose:** Compare 6-hour and 30-minute error ratios by route and status class.
- **Mitigate:** Reduce traffic to the failing route, roll back, or scale the workload after confirming capacity pressure.
- **Escalate:** Open an incident with the service owner if the warning persists for 30 minutes.
